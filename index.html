Privacy Policy

For the mobile game Block Wave Smash · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Teraverse ("we", "us", "the developer") handles information in connection with the mobile game Block Wave Smash (the "Game"). By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

The Game is designed to collect as little as possible. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos.

  • Nickname & scores (for the leaderboard and friend challenges): If you choose to use the online leaderboard or "Challenge a Friend", the nickname you enter and your game scores are sent to our cloud database (Google Firebase). Leaderboard nicknames and scores are publicly visible to other players; a challenge's nicknames and scores are visible to anyone who has that challenge's 6-character code. The nickname is optional and you can enter any name you like — you do not have to use your real name.
  • Advertising identifier: The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may access your device's advertising ID and related information to serve and measure ads. See "Third-party services" below.
  • On-device game data: Your progress, best score, coins, gems, settings and theme choice are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game removes this data.

How we use information

  • To operate the global leaderboard and show rankings.
  • To display and measure advertising.
  • To save your progress and preferences on your device.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

The Game uses these third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — https://policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob data disclosure.
  • Google Firebase (leaderboard database) — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information (for example, a nickname on the leaderboard), contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention & deletion

  • On-device data: removed when you uninstall the Game.
  • Leaderboard entries: to request removal of a nickname/score you submitted, email us (below) with the nickname and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to the leaderboard is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
